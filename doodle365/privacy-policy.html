Doodle365 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Doodle365 is a drawing and creativity application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the application.

2. Information Collection

Doodle365 is designed to provide drawing and creative activities. The information it handles depends on the features you use. If the app stores drawings, challenge progress, preferences, or other content on your device, that information may remain locally on your device.

The app's analytics, advertising, crash reporting, and third-party integrations must be reviewed to determine whether additional information is collected.

3. Personal Information

We do not intentionally request personal information unless a feature specifically requires it. If features that collect personal information are introduced, this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Third-Party Services

Doodle365 may use third-party services to support its operation. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies. Any services integrated into the published app should be identified in this section.

5. Data Storage and Security

Information stored locally is subject to the security of your device. Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Doodle365 does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
