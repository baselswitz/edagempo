Flipper: Learn & Play Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flipper: Learn & Play ("Flipper", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping the information associated with your use of Flipper secure and transparent.

1. Information We Collect

Flipper does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in order to use the App.

The App is designed to operate without requiring users to submit personal information directly to us.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Flipper stores certain app settings and progress information locally on your device so that the App can remember your preferences and continue your learning experience.

This may include:

  • Learning and game progress
  • Sound effects preferences
  • Background music preferences
  • Volume preferences
  • Other app preferences required for the App to function properly

This information is stored locally on your device and is not submitted to us as part of the normal operation of the App.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored locally by Flipper is used solely to provide and improve the functionality of the App, including remembering your settings and progress.

We do not use information stored locally by the App to identify individual users.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Flipper does not intentionally share personal information with third parties through the normal operation of the App.

5. Advertising

Flipper: Learn & Play does not require users to provide personal information for advertising purposes.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Flipper is not designed to require users to create an account or provide personal information for analytics or tracking purposes.

If this changes in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Flipper is designed as a learning and entertainment application. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the App.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications and review the privacy practices of applications used by their children.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Information stored locally by Flipper is maintained using the storage mechanisms provided by the device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, we aim to use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by the App.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

App settings and progress stored locally remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or removed.

You can generally remove locally stored App data by using the device's application settings to clear the App's data or by uninstalling the App, subject to the behavior of your device's operating system and backup services.

Because the information described in this section is stored locally, we do not maintain a separate copy of that information on our servers as part of the normal operation of Flipper.

10. Third-Party Services

Flipper may be distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play. Those platforms may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Flipper and does not govern the privacy practices of third-party platforms or services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
