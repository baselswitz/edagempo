FitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

FitFlow is a fitness and workout application designed to provide beginner-friendly training, exercise guidance, workout timers, and training alerts.

This Privacy Policy explains how FitFlow handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

FitFlow does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment information in order to use the current version of the app.

FitFlow does not intentionally collect or transmit personal or sensitive user information through the current version of the app.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

FitFlow may use your device's local storage for app functionality and preferences, such as settings required for the application to operate.

Information stored locally by FitFlow is not intentionally transmitted to us for advertising, profiling, or sale.

3. Workout and Exercise Information

FitFlow provides workout routines, exercise instructions, timers, and training alerts. The current version of FitFlow does not require you to provide health information, body measurements, medical information, or fitness records to use these features.

4. Sharing of Information

FitFlow does not sell, rent, or intentionally share your personal information with third parties.

The current version of FitFlow does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

5. Analytics and Advertising

The current version of FitFlow does not intentionally collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or user profiling.

6. Data Security

Because the current version of FitFlow does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information, there is no FitFlow account database containing your personal information.

We nevertheless take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary access to information and to keep the application and its supporting services appropriately maintained.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

FitFlow does not maintain a personal user account or personal profile in the current version of the app.

Information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the behavior of your device and Android operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

FitFlow is not specifically designed as a child-directed application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the app.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FitFlow's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
