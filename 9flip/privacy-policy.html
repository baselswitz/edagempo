9Flip Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

9Flip ("the App") is a visual memory game developed by Basel Labs. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how 9Flip handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

9Flip does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, or other directly identifying information.

The App does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

9Flip may store certain app settings and gameplay-related information locally on your device to provide features such as preferences and game progress.

This information remains on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to us.

3. Audio

9Flip may play music and sound effects as part of the gameplay experience. Audio used by the App is provided as part of the App and is not used to record or collect audio from you.

4. Advertising

9Flip does not contain third-party advertising.

5. Analytics and Tracking

9Flip does not intentionally use third-party analytics or tracking services to collect information about your activity.

6. Third-Party Services

9Flip does not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

7. Data Security

Because 9Flip does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a personal information database for users of the App.

8. Children's Privacy

9Flip does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
